# 🚀 Innovation, Design & Incubation MCQ Practice Web App

A self-contained, responsive, zero-dependency practice and test web app built from the course content of **Innovation, Product Design, Incubation & Entrepreneurship** (120 Questions across 12 Assignments), featuring a dedicated **⚡ Rapid Revision (Auto-Advance)** mode.

---

## ⚡ What's New: Rapid Revision Mode (Auto-Advance)

- **Automatic Question Transition**: When enabled, simply click your answer (or press <kbd>A</kbd>/<kbd>B</kbd>/<kbd>C</kbd>/<kbd>D</kbd>). The app displays instant feedback (green/red) and **automatically advances to the next question after a slight delay (~1.2s)**!
- **Adjustable Delay Speed**: Choose between `0.8s (Fast)`, `1.2s (Normal)`, or `2.0s (Relaxed)` directly from the home screen or settings.
- **Pause & Study**:
  - Click **⏸️ Pause & Study** or press <kbd>Space</kbd> at any point to stop the auto-timer and review the explanation as long as you need.
  - Click **▶️ Resume** or press <kbd>Space</kbd> again to continue auto-advancing.
- **Skip Ahead**: Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to skip the delay and advance instantly.
- **Toggle Anytime**: Toggle Auto-advance **ON / OFF** anytime with the top bar switch.

## 🎯 Practice Modes

- **⚡ Rapid Revision Mode**: Auto-advances after a slight delay for seamless high-speed testing.
- **🎯 Smart Mix**: Adaptive spaced repetition prioritizing weak questions, unattempted items, then review.
- **🎲 All Questions (Randomized)**: Complete randomized mock exam.
- **⚠️ Weak Only**: Direct drill on questions you got wrong.
- **✨ New / Unseen**: Focus only on unattempted questions.
- **⭐ Starred Questions**: Practice your custom bookmarked revision playlist.
